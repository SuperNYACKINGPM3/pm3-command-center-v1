# 00 — Master Architecture

## Canonical Purpose

PM3 Intelligence Atlas™ is the governed map of intelligence capabilities used to convert reality into verified decisions, coordinated action, durable assets, and improved future judgment.

## Control Sequence

1. **Human Authority** — retains accountability and final decision rights.
2. **Intent Intelligence™** — identifies the real objective and success criteria.
3. **Meta-Intelligence™** — selects the reasoning modules, sequence, evidence threshold, and review level.
4. **Wisdom Engine™** — verifies evidence, reasoning, risk, authority, alignment, and readiness.
5. **Execution Engine™** — converts approved decisions into action.
6. **Asset OS™** — preserves valuable outputs as governed assets.
7. **Learning Intelligence™** — converts outcomes into improved future capability.

## Governance Rules

- Capability does not equal permission.
- Permission does not equal authority.
- Recommendation does not equal decision.
- Automation does not remove accountability.
- High-consequence and hard-to-reverse actions require stronger evidence and explicit human approval.
- Canonical terms must retain source, owner, version, status, and change history.

## Decision Tiers

| Tier | Character | Default Control |
|---|---|---|
| T0 | Routine, reversible, low consequence | AI may execute within approved boundaries |
| T1 | Limited consequence | AI acts with logging and spot-checking |
| T2 | Material operational, financial, legal, or reputational effect | Full verification and human approval |
| T3 | Irreversible, constitutional, safety-critical, ownership-changing, or high-capital | Cooling-off, written rationale, expert review where applicable, and authorized human decision |

## Definition of Done

The Atlas is operational when every module has a canonical definition, activation trigger, inputs, outputs, evidence rules, failure modes, human authority boundary, metrics, relationships, SOP, and version record.
