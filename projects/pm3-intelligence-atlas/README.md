# PM3 Intelligence Atlas™

A governed reference architecture for human-AI intelligence, decision control, execution, learning, and asset creation.

> **Doctrine:** AI generates. Wisdom Engine™ verifies. Human judgment decides.

## Purpose

The Atlas defines the intelligence capabilities used across PM3, how they interact, when they activate, what evidence they require, and where human authority remains mandatory.

## Architecture

```text
Human Authority
    ↓
Intent Intelligence™
    ↓
Meta-Intelligence™
    ↓
Wisdom Engine™
    ↓
Perception → Evaluation → Allocation → Execution → Assets → Learning
    ↖───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
