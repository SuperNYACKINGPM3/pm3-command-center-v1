# 01–03 — Control Plane

## 01 Intent Intelligence™

**Canonical definition:** The discipline of determining the true objective, desired outcome, constraints, stakeholders, time horizon, and success criteria before reasoning or execution begins.

- **Primary question:** What is the real objective?
- **Activates when:** A request, project, decision, or conflict enters the system.
- **Inputs:** Stated request, context, stakeholders, constraints, goals, deadlines, definitions of success.
- **Outputs:** Intent profile, problem statement, scope, exclusions, success criteria, unresolved questions.
- **Failure modes:** Solving the wrong problem; optimizing a proxy; confusing urgency with importance; hidden stakeholder conflict.
- **Human authority:** The human owner confirms or revises consequential intent.
- **Core metric:** Percentage of completed work accepted without objective redefinition.

## 02 Meta-Intelligence™

**Canonical definition:** The orchestration discipline that determines how a problem should be analyzed by selecting the appropriate intelligence modules, domain expertise, evidence threshold, reasoning sequence, tools, and governance controls.

- **Primary question:** How should this problem be solved?
- **Activates when:** Intent is sufficiently clear or a task crosses domains or consequence thresholds.
- **Inputs:** Intent profile, context, consequence level, reversibility, available evidence, authority map.
- **Outputs:** Thinking plan, activated modules, source plan, review route, stop conditions, human gate.
- **Failure modes:** Over-analysis; wrong expert routing; tool-first reasoning; insufficient evidence; unnecessary complexity.
- **Human authority:** Humans approve T2/T3 analysis plans and exceptions.
- **Core metric:** Decision quality and cycle time compared with unstructured analysis.

## 03 Wisdom Engine™

**Canonical definition:** The governance and verification layer that tests provenance, evidence, reasoning, risk, authority, alignment, confidence, and readiness before action.

- **Primary question:** Is this trustworthy and authorized enough to proceed?
- **Activates when:** AI or humans propose a claim, decision, recommendation, policy, or action.
- **Inputs:** Proposed output, evidence set, source quality, risk profile, governing doctrine, authority record.
- **Outputs:** Verified, revise, quarantine, escalate, or reject verdict; confidence; conditions; evidence gaps; audit entry.
- **Failure modes:** Rubber-stamping; false certainty; authority confusion; missing provenance; weakening controls for convenience.
- **Human authority:** Human judgment owns final T2/T3 decisions and all written overrides.
- **Core metric:** Prevented errors, caught unsupported claims, override quality, and post-decision outcome review.

## Control Doctrine

> AI generates. Wisdom Engine™ verifies. Human judgment decides — and human attention is protected by tiered gates.
