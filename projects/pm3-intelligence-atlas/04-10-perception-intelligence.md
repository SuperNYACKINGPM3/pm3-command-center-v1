# 04–10 — Perception Intelligence

## 04 Semantic Intelligence™
Determines what words, symbols, statements, and representations mean in the relevant domain and context.
- **Primary question:** What does this mean here?
- **Outputs:** Contextual meaning, ambiguity flags, domain-specific interpretation.
- **Failure mode:** Treating one meaning as universal.

## 05 Definition Intelligence™
Establishes, preserves, governs, and versions canonical definitions.
- **Primary question:** What is the authoritative definition?
- **Outputs:** Canonical definition record, scope, source, owner, version, approved usage.
- **Failure mode:** Definition drift or competing unofficial meanings.

## 06 Pattern Intelligence™
Recognizes recurring structures, behaviors, sequences, and relationships that may explain or predict outcomes.
- **Primary question:** What repeats, and under what conditions?
- **Outputs:** Pattern statement, evidence instances, exceptions, predictive limits.
- **Failure mode:** Seeing patterns in noise or ignoring base rates.

## 07 Network Intelligence™
Maps relationships, dependencies, influence paths, clusters, bottlenecks, and leverage points across a system.
- **Primary question:** How are these connected?
- **Outputs:** Relationship graph, dependency map, central nodes, propagation risks.
- **Failure mode:** Studying isolated parts while missing system effects.

## 08 Provenance Intelligence™
Tracks origin, authorship, ownership, evidence lineage, transformations, approvals, and version history.
- **Primary question:** Where did this come from, and what happened to it?
- **Outputs:** Source chain, ownership record, transformation log, confidence, canonical status.
- **Failure mode:** Using copied, stale, synthetic, or altered material as authoritative.

## 09 Context Intelligence™
Interprets information within its historical, organizational, cultural, legal, economic, environmental, and situational conditions.
- **Primary question:** What surrounding conditions change the meaning or decision?
- **Outputs:** Relevant context set, boundary conditions, applicability statement.
- **Failure mode:** Correct information applied in the wrong setting.

## 10 Signal Intelligence™
Separates meaningful, decision-relevant indicators from noise, distraction, repetition, and manipulation.
- **Primary question:** What deserves attention now?
- **Outputs:** Ranked signals, evidence strength, urgency, relevance, monitoring triggers.
- **Failure mode:** Confusing volume, novelty, virality, or confidence with importance.

## Shared Perception Gate

Perception modules must identify ambiguity, missing context, provenance weakness, and signal uncertainty before evaluation begins. A fluent interpretation is not automatically a verified interpretation.
