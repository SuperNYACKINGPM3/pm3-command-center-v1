Hey {{first_name}},

Ask most traders why they blew an account and you'll hear about a bad setup, a fake breakout, or a
missed signal.

That's rarely the real story.

The real story is usually one of these:

- **No risk plan.** They entered without a predefined stop, target, or invalidation point.
- **FOMO.** They chased a move that had already happened.
- **Revenge trading.** They tried to win back a loss immediately, compounding one mistake into
  several.
- **Overtrading.** More trades felt like more control. It was actually more noise.
- **Ignoring stops.** A stop got moved "just this once" — and that once became the pattern.
- **Position size errors.** The setup was fine. The size was not.

Notice what's missing from that list: indicators, strategies, and signals.

None of these are technical problems. They're decision problems. Which is exactly why more
indicators never fixes them — and why PM3 is built around decisions, not signals.
