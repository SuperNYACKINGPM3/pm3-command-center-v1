Hey {{first_name}},

Here's an uncomfortable truth: a great setup with the wrong position size can still end a trading
account. A mediocre setup with the right size almost never will.

Position size is where risk actually lives.

The basic framework PM3 teaches:

1. **Decide your risk per trade** — a fixed percentage of your account, not a fixed dollar amount
   that "feels right."
2. **Find your stop distance** — the price move that proves your thesis wrong.
3. **Calculate size from those two numbers** — not from how confident you feel.

When size is calculated instead of guessed, a losing trade becomes a data point, not a crisis. That's
the entire goal: no single trade should be able to meaningfully damage your account.

The Command Center includes a Position Size Calculator that does this math for you, every time —
so it stops being a decision you have to remember to make under pressure.
