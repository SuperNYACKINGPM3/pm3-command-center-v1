Hey {{first_name}},

Everything inside PM3 runs through one filter:

**AI generates. Wisdom verifies. Human judgment decides.**

Here's what that actually means in practice:

**1. AI generates.**
AI can scan markets and surface setups faster than any human — but a fast idea is not the same as
a good one. Speed without a filter just means faster mistakes.

**2. Wisdom verifies.**
Before any idea is actionable, it has to pass through rules: your risk parameters, your known
biases, your account's actual constraints. This is the layer most traders skip — and it's the one
that matters most.

**3. Human judgment decides.**
The final decision is always yours. AI narrows the field and removes noise. It does not, and should
not, pull the trigger for you.

Most trading tools stop at step one. PM3 is built around the idea that step one is the easy part.
