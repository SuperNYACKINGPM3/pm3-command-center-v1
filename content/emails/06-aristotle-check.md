Hey {{first_name}},

We named this one after the guy who spent a career insisting that good outcomes come from good
reasoning, not luck. It felt appropriate.

**The Aristotle Check™** is the pre-trade filter inside the Command Center. Before a trade is
allowed to happen, you have to answer four questions:

1. **What is my thesis?** State it in one sentence. If you can't, you don't have one yet.
2. **What proves me wrong?** Your invalidation point — the exact condition that ends the trade.
3. **What is my size, based on my risk rule?** Not a guess. A calculation.
4. **What is my exit, win or lose?** Decided before entry, not improvised after.

That's it. Four questions, every time, no exceptions for setups that "feel obvious."

Most blown accounts don't come from a single bad decision — they come from skipping this exact
kind of check on the trade that felt too good to slow down for.

You now have the full doctrine: AI generates, wisdom verifies, human judgment decides — backed by a
risk plan, a sizing method, and a pre-trade check that holds you to both.

That's the system. The rest is repetition.

Welcome to the Command Center.
